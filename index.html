Everyone. The course is open to all and no prior trading or finance experience is needed.

No. PROFIT+ is completely free.

Six days, starting 28th September, one topic per day from Market Foundation to the Final Challenge.

Yes. Complete the course to receive the PROFIT+ Trading Foundation Program certificate from the Finance & Economics Club, IIT Guwahati.

In the course WhatsApp group. Register first, then join the group so you don't miss any announcements.
